🎵 clapback

A public commons of CLAP audio embeddings — 512-dimensional vectors describing what a recording sounds like. Expensive to compute, trivial to compare, so worth computing once and sharing.

Keyed on anonymised SHA256 fingerprint hashes: no titles, no artists, no filenames. Familiar is its first client and largest contributor, and is not its owner — anything can contribute.
