🎵 The shape of the corpus

Every embedding in the commons, projected from 512 dimensions to two. Points that sit together sounded alike to the model. Nothing here is a title or an artist — the corpus holds neither.

What you are looking at

512 numbers per recording, reduced to two by UMAP so they fit on a screen. The reduction is lossy and not unique: distances between distant clusters mean much less than the fact that a cluster is a cluster, and a different random seed would rotate the whole picture. What survives is the neighbourhood structure — which recordings the model considered close to which.

There are no labels because there is nothing to label with. The corpus is keyed on the SHA256 of an AcoustID fingerprint and holds no titles, artists or filenames, so a point can be near other points and still not be nameable. That is the privacy property working as intended rather than a missing feature.

Reproduce it

The projection is a committed artifact with a date on it, not something the server computes — UMAP pulls in numba and llvmlite, and the instance is sized for a vector index rather than a toolchain. packages/server/scripts/build_map.py regenerates it, and the seed is pinned so the same corpus gives the same picture.
